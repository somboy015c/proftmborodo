Profile

A lawyer who learned the business of boards

Alhaji Tijjani Mohammed Borodo began his career in 1979 as a Pupil State Counsel at the Kano State Ministry of Justice. He rose to Director of Public Prosecutions for the old Kano State, which today comprises Kano and Jigawa, while still at a young age.

In 1988 he moved to FirstBank of Nigeria, held several managerial positions, and in June 1999 became its Company Secretary. In 2012 he became the first Company Secretary of FBN Holdings Plc and guided the holding company onto the Premium Board of the Nigerian Stock Exchange.

After retiring from the group in October 2017, he founded his own law firm and deepened his work with the Institute of Directors, which elected him its president in 2023. Colleagues describe a bridge-builder who commands respect for his generosity and humility.

Career timeline

From the Kano Ministry of Justice to the boardroom

Four decades of public service, banking and governance.

  1. 1979

    Pupil State Counsel, Kano

    Starts at the Ministry of Justice, Kano State, after graduating in law from Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria. Called to the Bar in July 1980.

  2. 1986

    Director of Public Prosecutions

    Reaches the top prosecutorial office of old Kano State, covering today's Kano and Jigawa, at a young age.

  3. 1988

    Joins FirstBank of Nigeria

    Leaves public service for banking and takes on a series of managerial positions.

  4. June 1999

    Company Secretary, FirstBank

    Oversees the Secretariat and serves as Secretary to the Board and to the Annual General Meeting for 14 years.

  5. Sept 2012

    Pioneer Company Secretary, FBN Holdings

    Leads the company secretarial function of the new holding company and its admission to the Premium Board of the Nigerian Stock Exchange.

  6. Oct 2017

    Retires after 29 years

    Turns to private practice as founder and Principal Partner of Tijjani M. Borodo & Associates.

  7. March 2021

    Publishes A Journey in Leadership

    Launches his 180-page autobiography in Ikoyi, Lagos, with proceeds supporting the Hajjiya Larai Borodo Foundation.

  8. June 2023

    Elected President, CIoD Nigeria

    Chosen at the 39th Annual General Meeting and later invested as 18th President and Chairman of the Governing Council.

The book

A Journey in Leadership

A 180-page autobiography tracing his path as a child, scholar, prosecutor, banker and governance advocate.

The book follows his upbringing, his grounding in Arabic school, his student days at ABU Zaria, his years at the Kano Ministry of Justice, and his long service at FirstBank.

Beyond the boardroom

Service beyond the boardroom

Proceeds from the book launch went to the Hajjiya Larai Borodo Foundation, established by his wife to support less-privileged children. He credits family, colleagues and faith for his achievements.

Fellow executives have described him as a detribalised Nigerian and bridge-builder, and a former FirstBank chairman praised his selflessness in service.

I remain grateful for my eventful career, across the public and private sectors, as these prepared me for the legal profession I have always loved.Paraphrased from Alhaji Tijjani M. Borodo at the launch of his autobiography
